Who this is for

This is for you if you are in perimenopause and:

  • You are eating less than you ever have, and your belly is bigger than it was 2 years ago.
  • You are awake at 2 or 3 most nights, and wrecked by 11 the next morning.
  • You are bloated most days, and you are having hot flashes and night sweats.
  • You lose the word halfway through a sentence in a meeting, and you read the same email 3 times.
  • You have cut the bread, the wine, the fruit, and none of it moved anything.
  • You are snapping at people you love and you do not recognise yourself.
  • You have started saying no to things because of what you would have to wear.

This is not for you if:

  • You are not in perimenopause.
  • You are on a GLP-1. Ozempic, Mounjaro, Wegovy.
  • Your thyroid condition is untreated. See your doctor first.
  • You are not willing to eat more than you are eating now.
  • You will not take your training down.
  • You are looking for medical treatment. I am a coach, not a doctor.

I've tried 4 different things and none of them worked. Why would this?

Because all 4 of them told you to eat less. That's the one thing everybody has told you since this started, and it's the thing that stopped working. I'm going to have you eat more and train less. That's the opposite, and that's why this one is different.

I'm scared that eating more is going to make it worse.

You've been cutting for 2 years, so of course more food sounds like the wrong answer. But the cutting is what got you here. The amount I give you is worked out for your body, and I adjust it as we go. You're not guessing and you're not doing it on your own.

It's a group coaching program. What if I get lost in it?

You won't, because you don't wait 2 weeks for me. The group calls are every 2 weeks, but you've got me in the app every day in between. Something comes up on a Tuesday, you message me Tuesday. Something comes up every day that week, you message me every day. You get the group, and you get me.

I'm not sure I'm actually in perimenopause.

You don't need a test and most women don't have one. If your periods have changed, your sleep has changed, and your body is behaving differently than it did at 35, that's enough to start. The intake form covers it, and if I don't think this is what's going on with you, I'll tell you straight.

Do I have to track my food?

Yes. Part of losing fat in perimenopause is eating the right amount for your body, and I need to see what you're eating now to work out what that is. I'll show you how, and it won't take over your life.

I've got a bad knee. Or a shoulder. Or a back.

Everything has a modified version. Tell me on your intake form what's going on and you'll get the version that works for your body. You don't have to be injury free to do this.

I'm buying in December but we don't start until January 11. Why the wait?

Because December is the worst month to start this. Christmas, travel, something on every weekend. Start you on the 8th and you'd spend 3 weeks fighting your own calendar. So you enjoy December, and you walk into January already signed up and ready to go.

Will I gain it back when the 3 months are over?
